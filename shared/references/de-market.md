# German Comp and Contract Terms

A German offer cannot be compared to a US one by base salary alone. Half the value is in structures that do not exist in American compensation, and a tool that ignores them will tell the candidate a worse offer is better.

Always reason in **Jahresbrutto** (annual gross). Never in monthly, never in net — net depends on Steuerklasse, church tax, Bundesland and health insurance, and quoting it implies a precision that is not there.

## Compensation components

| Term | What it is | Effect on comparison |
|---|---|---|
| **13. Monatsgehalt / Weihnachtsgeld** | An extra month's salary, usually paid in November | Multiply by 13, not 12. Tarif industries sometimes pay 13.5 or 14. Missing this is the single most common German comp mistake. |
| **Urlaubsgeld** | Holiday bonus, separate from the 13th | Add it. |
| **Urlaub** | Paid leave. Statutory minimum 20 days on a 5-day week; **25–30 is the actual market standard** | Under 28 days is below tech market rate and is negotiable. |
| **VWL** (Vermögenswirksame Leistungen) | Employer contribution to a savings plan, often ~€40/month | Small but real. |
| **bAV** (Betriebliche Altersvorsorge) | Occupational pension | Can be several hundred euros a month. Materially changes a comparison. |
| **Tarifvertrag** (TVöD, TV-L, IG Metall, IG BCE) | Collective agreement fixing pay bands and raises | Less negotiating room, more certainty: defined progression, defined hours, strong protections. Public sector roles quote a band like `E13` or `TVöD 14` — decode it rather than treating it as unknown. |
| **Betriebsrat** | Works council | A stability signal, strong in Mittelstand and corporates. Say on dismissal, hours, and shift patterns. |
| **Jobticket / Deutschlandticket, Essenszuschuss, Jobrad** | Transit, meal and bike benefits | Common, modest, quantifiable. |
| **Firmenwagen** | Company car | Taxed as a benefit in kind. Common in sales and senior Mittelstand roles. |
| **RSUs / equity** | Rare outside startups and US subsidiaries | Do not assume it exists. |

## Contract structures

| Term | Meaning | Flag if |
|---|---|---|
| **Unbefristet** | Permanent | The default to want. |
| **Befristet** | Fixed-term | Always surface the end date and whether it can convert. Common in academia and the public sector. |
| **Probezeit** | Probation, usually 6 months, with a shortened 2-week notice period | Market standard. Only flag if longer than 6 months. |
| **Kündigungsfrist** | Notice period after probation, typically 1–3 months, sometimes to month or quarter end | **Drives the Eintrittstermin on every application.** Read it from the candidate's profile and compute a realistic start date. |
| **Festanstellung vs. Freie Mitarbeit** | Employment vs. contracting | Freelance day rates in DACH generally run well above the equivalent employed hourly cost, because the contractor carries social insurance, holiday, sickness, acquisition and tax-advisor costs. Check **Scheinselbstständigkeit** risk for single-client contracting. |
| **Arbeitnehmerüberlassung** | Temp leasing | See `de-job-boards.md`. Label it. |
| **Vertrauensarbeitszeit / Gleitzeit / Kernarbeitszeit** | Trust-based / flex / core hours | Tells you a lot about the culture. |
| **Überstundenregelung** | Overtime terms | „Überstunden sind mit dem Gehalt abgegolten" means unpaid overtime is contractually expected. Worth flagging. |

## Salary research

Use, in rough order of usefulness for Germany: **StepStone Gehaltsreport**, **kununu Gehalt**, **Gehalt.de**, **Glassdoor**, **Levels.fyi** (only for international tech), and the **Entgelttabellen** for any Tarif role — public-sector bands are published, so a TVöD figure can be looked up exactly rather than estimated.

Regional spread is large. Munich, Frankfurt and Stuttgart pay well above Leipzig or Dresden, and cost of living tracks it unevenly.

## Stating a Gehaltsvorstellung

German ads often *require* a salary expectation, and omitting it when asked gets applications filtered.

- Give a single number or a tight band, in Jahresbrutto: `Meine Gehaltsvorstellung liegt bei 75.000 EUR brutto p.a.`
- Add flexibility without undercutting: `Bei der Struktur bin ich flexibel — entscheidend ist das Gesamtpaket.`
- Anchor on researched market data for the title and region, not on the candidate's current salary.
- If the ad does **not** ask, do not volunteer it.

Take the target from `profile.md`. If it is missing, ask once, store it, and never guess a number on the candidate's behalf — an invented figure in a submitted application is a real, lasting cost to them.
