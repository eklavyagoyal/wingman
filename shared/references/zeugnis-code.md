# Arbeitszeugnis: Reading the Code

A German employment reference (**Arbeitszeugnis**) is legally required to be both *truthful* and *benevolent* (§109 GewO: "wahr und wohlwollend"). Those two duties conflict, so German employers evolved a coded register: the letter reads warmly and grades you precisely. Every HR person in Germany reads the code. Most candidates do not.

**Two jobs for this reference:**
1. **Audit the candidate's own Zeugnisse before they get attached.** A politely-worded Zeugnis can carry a grade of 4 or a conduct red flag. The candidate has a legal right to demand correction — but only if they know.
2. **Mine Zeugnisse for CV evidence.** They often contain specific responsibilities and results the candidate forgot to put on their CV.

---

## The Leistung (performance) scale

This is a graded formula. The words are near-identical; the grade is not. Match the phrasing exactly — `vollsten` vs `vollen` and the presence of `stets` are the whole signal.

| Formulation | Grade | Note |
|---|---|---|
| „…**stets** zu unserer **vollsten** Zufriedenheit" | **1 — sehr gut** | The only unambiguously excellent wording. |
| „…zu unserer **vollsten** Zufriedenheit" *(no `stets`)* | **2 — gut** | Dropping `stets` drops a grade. |
| „…**stets** zu unserer **vollen** Zufriedenheit" | **2 — gut** | |
| „…zu unserer **vollen** Zufriedenheit" | **3 — befriedigend** | The most common wording. Middling, not good. |
| „…**im Großen und Ganzen** zu unserer Zufriedenheit" | **4 — ausreichend** | Damaging. |
| „…hat sich **bemüht**" / „war bemüht, den Anforderungen gerecht zu werden" | **5 — mangelhaft** | Reads kind, means *failed*. Never attach this unchallenged. |
| „…hat die Aufgaben mit großem Fleiß und Interesse erledigt" | **5** | Effort praised, results conspicuously absent. |

**Rule of thumb:** `stets` = consistently, `vollste` = superlative. Both present → 1. Neither → 3 or worse.

## The Verhalten / Führung (conduct) scale

Read **who is named**, not just the adjective. The standard full formula covers both superiors and colleagues:

> „Sein Verhalten gegenüber **Vorgesetzten und Kollegen** war stets **vorbildlich**."

| Signal | Reads as |
|---|---|
| Vorgesetzten **and** Kollegen, `stets vorbildlich` / `einwandfrei` | Very good. |
| **Kollegen only — Vorgesetzte omitted** | Conflict with management. A deliberate omission, and recruiters catch it. |
| **Vorgesetzten only — Kollegen omitted** | Did not work well in the team. |
| Conduct sentence **missing entirely** | Loud silence. Worse than a mediocre grade. |
| Order reversed (`Kollegen und Vorgesetzten`) | Weak signal, sometimes read as a slight. |

## Coded phrases that mean the opposite of what they say

| Phrase | Actual meaning |
|---|---|
| „Er war für seine **Geselligkeit** bekannt" | Drinking problem. |
| „…**trug zur Verbesserung des Betriebsklimas** bei" | Flirtatious, or the same as above. |
| „…zeigte **Verständnis für seine Arbeit**" | Understood the work; did not do it. |
| „Durch seine **Pünktlichkeit** war er ein Vorbild" | There was nothing else positive to say. |
| „…war **mit Umsicht** tätig" | Slow. |
| „…hat **alle Aufgaben delegiert**" | Lazy. |
| „…**bewährte** sich in dem ihm übertragenen Aufgabenkreis" | Bare-minimum performer. |
| „Wir lernten ihn als **umtriebigen** Mitarbeiter kennen" | Chaotic, self-promoting. |
| „…mit **Eigeninitiative**, die stets Anerkennung fand" *(neutral-sounding, no result)* | Watch for effort words with no outcome — that pattern *is* the code. |

## The Schlussformel — check the ending

The closing (Dank-, Bedauerns- und Zukunftsformel) is where the temperature shows. Full positive version:

> „Wir bedanken uns für die stets gute Zusammenarbeit und **bedauern** sein Ausscheiden. Für die Zukunft wünschen wir ihm alles Gute und weiterhin viel Erfolg."

| What is there | Reads as |
|---|---|
| Thanks + `bedauern` + good wishes | Warm, genuine. |
| Thanks + good wishes, **no `bedauern`** | They were content to see you go. |
| Good wishes only | Cool. |
| **No closing formula at all** | Deliberate. Reads as a bad separation. |
| „…verließ uns **auf eigenen Wunsch**" | You resigned. Good, neutral fact. |
| „…verließ uns **im gegenseitigen Einvernehmen**" | Negotiated exit — usually pushed. |
| „…**musste** wir uns trennen" / „aus betriebsbedingten Gründen" | Redundancy (betriebsbedingt is neutral and worth stating plainly). |

## Einfaches vs. qualifiziertes Zeugnis

- **Einfaches Zeugnis** — dates, role, duties only. No assessment.
- **Qualifiziertes Zeugnis** — adds Leistung and Verhalten. **This is what employers expect**, and the employee has a right to request it.

Receiving only an einfaches Zeugnis after a multi-year role is itself a signal. If the candidate has one, tell them they can request a qualifiziertes one.

## Workflow: auditing a candidate's Zeugnis

1. Read the Zeugnis (file or pasted text).
2. Locate the Leistung sentence → grade it against the table. Quote the exact wording back.
3. Locate the Verhalten sentence → check **who is named**.
4. Check the Schlussformel for thanks / `bedauern` / good wishes.
5. Scan for coded phrases.
6. Report a grade **with the quoted evidence**, never a bare verdict:

```
Arbeitszeugnis - [Company], [dates]

Leistung:  Grade 3 (befriedigend)
  "...die ihm übertragenen Aufgaben zu unserer vollen Zufriedenheit erledigt"
  -> "vollen" not "vollsten", and no "stets". Two steps below top band.

Verhalten: Concern
  "Sein Verhalten gegenuber Kollegen war einwandfrei."
  -> Vorgesetzte are not mentioned. A German recruiter reads this as
     friction with management.

Schluss:   Cool
  Good wishes present, but no thanks and no "bedauern".

Verdict: this Zeugnis works against you. You have a legal right under
§109 GewO to a truthful and benevolent reference. Options:
  1. Ask the former employer to revise to "stets zu unserer vollen
     Zufriedenheit" and to include Vorgesetzte in the conduct sentence.
  2. Attach it anyway - grade 3 is common and not disqualifying.
  3. Attach only the Abschlusszeugnis and note "weitere Zeugnisse werden
     auf Wunsch nachgereicht."

I am not a lawyer. For a contested correction, a Fachanwalt für
Arbeitsrecht or your union handles this routinely and cheaply.
```

**Never rewrite a Zeugnis.** It is a document authored and signed by a third party. Wingman audits it, extracts evidence from it, and advises the candidate on their options. Producing an altered version of an employer's signed reference would be forgery — decline that, always, and say why.

**Never overstate the legal position.** The right to a benevolent reference is real; a *specific grade* is not owed, and disputes over wording turn on facts. Advise, cite §109 GewO, and point to a lawyer or union for anything contested.
